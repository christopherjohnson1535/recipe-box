# Finding Recipes

## The top of every screen
A dark band runs across the top of every signed-in screen: today's date on your device's clock, the season and how many
recipes your household has (left out if the count can't be read), with **Sign out** on the right. Under it are the
Recipe Box name and the section links, with a thin double line below.

## The home page
The top of the home page is set like a magazine cover: a **cover story**, a recipe with a photo that you haven't touched in a while, with **Start
cooking** and **Read the recipe**. Under it a slow **In season** ticker lists produce for the time of year (hover over it,
tab onto it or press **Pause** to stop it; on a touch screen tap it). Below are the recipe cards, with a numbered **Also in this issue** list and a **Haven't
cooked in a while** panel beside them (under them on an iPad held upright or a phone). While you search or pick a tag,
the cover, ticker and side panels step aside and only the matching recipes show. If your device is set to reduce
motion, the ticker stands still and cards don't move.

## Searching
The home page searches as you type: pause for a moment and the list updates. Enter or **Search** also works, and the
browser's Back button returns to your previous search.

## What search looks at
Titles, descriptions, notes, **tags** and **ingredients**, so "dinner" finds recipes tagged dinner and "buttermilk" finds every recipe that uses it. A half-typed word
works too: "lasag" finds "Weeknight lasagne". The line under the box says how many recipes match.

## Tricks
- `"beef mince"` in quotes finds that exact phrase.
- `soup -tomato` leaves out recipes that mention tomato.
- `soup or stew` finds either.

## Tags
Tap a tag under the search box to show only those recipes; the number is how many recipes carry it. On a phone the
tags fold into one **Tag** row: tap it to open the list. Tags and search
combine: search "pie" with the tag *dessert* to see dessert pies. Tap **All** to drop the tag, or **Clear search** on
the empty-results page to start over.

## Sorting
The menu next to the box offers **Best match** (only while searching), **Newest**, **A–Z** and **Quickest** (shortest
total time first; recipes with no time are last). The choice is kept in the page address, so you can bookmark it.
