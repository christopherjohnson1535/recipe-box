# Appearance and layout

How Recipe Box looks and where things are. The same pages work on a phone, an iPad and a computer; wider screens use
the room for a second column, never for longer lines of text.

## Day and evening
Recipe Box has two sets of colours: **day** (pale paper, charcoal text, a deep saffron accent) and **evening** (near-black
slate with a brighter saffron). **Settings → Appearance** chooses when each shows, on that device only:
- **By the clock: evening colours after 7 pm** (the default), day from 7 am.
- **Always day** or **Always evening**.
- **Follow this device**: use the light or dark setting of the iPad or computer.

Cooking mode is always in the evening colours.

## On every page
A dark strip at the top shows today's date, the season and how many recipes the household has, with **Sign out** on the
right. Under it are the Recipe Box name and the sections: **Recipes**, **Groceries** (with how many items are left to
buy), **Add**, **Import** and **Settings**, with a thin double line below. The strip and the line are left out when you
print.

## Wide screens
From about 1280 pixels across (a computer, or an iPad held sideways in a large window) pages gain a second column:
- **Add a recipe**, **Edit** and **Import**: the form on the left, a short panel of tips on the right. While an import is
  running or being checked, the photo or page sits beside the draft instead.
- **Groceries**: the list on the left; the recipes on the list, **Send to…** and **Clear ticked** / **Empty list** on the right.
- **Settings**: your account, Google sign-in and the household on the left; photos, export, Google Tasks, appearance, saving
  from Safari and AI import on the right.
- **A recipe** (about 1440 pixels and up): ingredients, method and the recipe's note side by side.

Narrower than that, everything is one column in the same order, and the tip panels are left out on a phone.

## Saving a recipe
When a new recipe is saved a round **In the box · Saved** stamp appears in the corner for a moment. Edits show a small
**Saved.** message instead.
