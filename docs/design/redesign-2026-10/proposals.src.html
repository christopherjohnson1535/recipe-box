Braised Short Ribs with Gremolata

Deep, winey, falling off the bone, then woken up with lemon, parsley and raw garlic.

Added by Chris · Edited by Sam, Oct 2 · Source: seriouseats.com
From Chris's photo, Mar 14
4Serves
20minPrep
3hrCook
3:20Total

Ingredients

  • bone-in short ribs
  • kosher salt
  • olive oil
  • large onion, diced
  • carrots, diced
  • tomato paste
  • dry red wine
  • beef stock
  • For the gremolata
  • parsley, finely chopped
  • lemon, zested
  • garlic, grated

Method

6 steps
1

Heat the oven to 300°F. Season the ribs all over with salt and let them sit while the pot heats.

2

Brown the ribs in olive oil in batches, turning, until deeply browned on every side 15 min. Don't crowd the pot.

S"Our Dutch oven only fits four at a time. Do two rounds, it's worth it." — Sam, Oct 2
3

Pour off all but a spoonful of fat. Add the onion and carrots and cook until soft 12 min, then stir in the tomato paste.

"Let it rest overnight. It's better on Monday."Chris's note on this recipe

PROPOSAL D · IMPORT

Import feels like developing a print

The wait while the AI reads a photo becomes the show.

  • Anything uncertain is flagged in mustard, not silently guessed
  • Photo, link and paste are three doors into the same darkroom

PROPOSAL E · DETAIL KIT

Motion and details, with rules

A small kit so every screen gets the same polish. Motion only marks a change; nothing bounces for fun.

  • Card-to-page: the plate grows into the hero (View Transitions)
  • Save gets a rubber-stamp moment and an undo toast
  • Hand-drawn spot icons for tags and empty states
  • Day and Evening editions switch with the clock
  • Content arrives in a short stagger; loading shows the page's shape
